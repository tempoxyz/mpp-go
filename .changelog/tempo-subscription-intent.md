Support the Tempo `subscription` intent (draft-tempo-subscription-00), as mppx's `tempo.subscription`. Clients sign a key authorization scoped to the Challenge's amount, currency, recipient, period, expiry and server access key with `chargeclient.NewSubscription`, which requires a `ValidateRequest` to authorize what it signs. Servers issue subscription Challenges, verify the key authorization, activate the subscription with a receipt carrying its `subscriptionId`, reuse it on later requests and renew overdue periods under the same `subscriptionId` with `chargeserver.NewSubscription`, through custom `Activate`/`Renew` hooks or automatic on-chain billing with a server-owned access key. Adds `SubscriptionStore`, whose keys follow mppx's except that a lookup key starting with `address:` or `lookup:` stores its access key under `tempo:subscription:access-key:lookup:` plus the key, so no lookup key can land on the access key address index (mppx stores them as is, where an `address:` lookup key collides with it), the `tempo.AtomicStore` interface (implemented by `MemoryStore` and `RedisStore`, whose `CompareAndSwap` writes never expire) and `mpp.IntentSubscription`. Automatic billing keeps each signed payment with its activation or renewal lock until its outcome is known, so a payment whose receipt could not be read is resolved (or broadcast again) by the next attempt instead of charged again; each automatic payment carries a `valid_before` five minutes out, and a pending payment counts as failed only once it reverted, or the chain is past its `valid_before` with no receipt, never on a receipt read error or a refused rebroadcast, and `Subscribe` refuses to issue a Challenge that expires no earlier than the subscription. Expiry is proven at one block: one whose timestamp is past `valid_before` and at which the payer's nonce is still the payment's. When the nonce there is already past the payment's, the server finds the block that used it, reading the payer's nonce at explicit block numbers (galloping back from that block, then bisecting, at most 64 reads), and reads that block's transaction hashes: a block without the payment proves another transaction took its nonce, so the payment failed and the period is billed again; a block with it proves the payment landed, so it settles from its receipt and, while that receipt cannot be read (pruned, or a lagging replica), keeps the period locked instead of billing it twice. Any read error leaves the outcome unknown. A transfer included without crediting the recipient (held by a receive policy) moved the payer's funds, so its activation or period is committed as paid with the transaction hash as its `Reference`, marked `Held` and reported to the new `OnPaymentHeld` hook, rather than freed and billed again as mppx does. Every write after a lock is taken is fenced (records are created once, and the lookup key and replaced subscriptions change only by compare-and-swap), so an attempt that lost its lock never overwrites what another committed; a custom `Activate` that reuses a `SubscriptionID` replaces that ID's ended record (or its lookup key's current one), and a live record of another lookup key keeps the settled activation for a later attempt instead of dropping it. A paid activation or period, including one a later attempt recovers or one a concurrent activation superseded, is recorded and reported to `OnActivated` or `OnRenewed` exactly once. Activation claims are released when an attempt paid nothing, and with `SubscriptionStoreOptions.ExpireCredentialClaims` (opt-in, as the charge intent's `ExpireReplayKeys`, since `RedisStore` needs Redis 6.2 or later for it) expire with their Challenge on a `tempo.ExpiringStore`. Retry Challenges name only an access key this server issued, `Verify` rejects an access key that is not the lookup key's, and a subscription a custom `Activate` settled renews only through `Renew`. Subscription requests reject a `chainId` that is not positive. A request without a Credential gets a fresh Challenge only when a renewal failed before any payment went out (`ErrSubscriptionPaymentNotSent`, which a custom `Renew` wraps when it charged nothing); an activation that paid keeps its settled record until a later attempt commits it. The subscription client rejects a Challenge whose chain differs from a configured `ChainID`, as the charge client does.
