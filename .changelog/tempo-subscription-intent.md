Support the Tempo `subscription` intent (draft-tempo-subscription-00), as mppx's `tempo.subscription`. Clients sign a key authorization scoped to the Challenge's amount, currency, recipient, period, expiry and server access key with `chargeclient.NewSubscription`, which requires a `ValidateRequest` to authorize what it signs. Servers issue subscription Challenges, verify the key authorization, activate the subscription with a receipt carrying its `subscriptionId`, reuse it on later requests and renew overdue periods under the same `subscriptionId` with `chargeserver.NewSubscription`, through custom `Activate`/`Renew` hooks or automatic on-chain billing with a server-owned access key. Adds `SubscriptionStore`, the `tempo.AtomicStore` interface (implemented by `MemoryStore` and `RedisStore`, whose `CompareAndSwap` writes never expire) and `mpp.IntentSubscription`. Automatic billing keeps each signed payment with its activation or renewal lock until its outcome is known, so a payment whose receipt could not be read is resolved (or broadcast again) by the next attempt instead of charged again; each automatic payment carries a `valid_before` five minutes out, and a pending payment counts as failed only once it reverted, or the chain is past its `valid_before` with no receipt, never on a receipt read error or a refused rebroadcast, and `Subscribe` refuses to issue a Challenge that expires no earlier than the subscription. Expiry is proven at one block: one whose timestamp is past `valid_before` and at which the payer's nonce is still the payment's. A nonce another transaction used, with no receipt for the payment, fails it only when a later attempt at least two minutes after the first one that saw it still finds no receipt, so a lagging RPC replica cannot fake it and a reused nonce cannot lock the period for good. A transfer included without crediting the recipient (held by a receive policy) moved the payer's funds, so its activation or period is committed as paid with the transaction hash as its `Reference`, marked `Held` and reported to the new `OnPaymentHeld` hook, rather than freed and billed again as mppx does. Every write after a lock is taken is fenced (records are created once, and the lookup key and replaced subscriptions change only by compare-and-swap), so an attempt that lost its lock never overwrites what another committed; a custom `Activate` that reuses a `SubscriptionID` replaces that ID's ended record (or its lookup key's current one), and a live record of another lookup key keeps the settled activation for a later attempt instead of dropping it. A paid activation or period, including one a later attempt recovers or one a concurrent activation superseded, is recorded and reported to `OnActivated` or `OnRenewed` exactly once. Activation claims are released when an attempt paid nothing, and with `SubscriptionStoreOptions.ExpireCredentialClaims` (opt-in, as the charge intent's `ExpireReplayKeys`, since `RedisStore` needs Redis 6.2 or later for it) expire with their Challenge on a `tempo.ExpiringStore`. Retry Challenges name only an access key this server issued, `Verify` rejects an access key that is not the lookup key's, and a subscription a custom `Activate` settled renews only through `Renew`. Subscription requests reject a `chainId` that is not positive. A request without a Credential gets a fresh Challenge only when a renewal failed before any payment went out (`ErrSubscriptionPaymentNotSent`, which a custom `Renew` wraps when it charged nothing); an activation that paid keeps its settled record until a later attempt commits it. The subscription client rejects a Challenge whose chain differs from a configured `ChainID`, as the charge client does.
