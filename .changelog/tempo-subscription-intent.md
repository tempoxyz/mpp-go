Support the Tempo `subscription` intent (draft-tempo-subscription-00), as mppx's `tempo.subscription`. Clients sign a key authorization scoped to the Challenge's amount, currency, recipient, period, expiry and server access key with `chargeclient.NewSubscription`, which requires a `ValidateRequest` to authorize what it signs. Servers issue subscription Challenges, verify the key authorization, activate the subscription with a receipt carrying its `subscriptionId`, reuse it on later requests and renew overdue periods under the same `subscriptionId` with `chargeserver.NewSubscription`, through custom `Activate`/`Renew` hooks or automatic on-chain billing with a server-owned access key. Adds `SubscriptionStore`, the `tempo.AtomicStore` interface (implemented by `MemoryStore` and `RedisStore`, whose `CompareAndSwap` writes never expire) and `mpp.IntentSubscription`. Automatic billing keeps each signed payment with its activation or renewal lock until its outcome is known, so a payment whose receipt could not be read is resolved (or broadcast again) by the next attempt instead of charged again; each automatic payment carries a `valid_before` five minutes out, and a pending payment counts as failed only once it reverted, or the chain is past its `valid_before` with no receipt, never on a receipt read error or a refused rebroadcast, and `Subscribe` refuses to issue a Challenge that expires no earlier than the subscription. A request without a Credential gets a fresh Challenge only when a renewal failed before any payment went out (`ErrSubscriptionPaymentNotSent`, which a custom `Renew` wraps when it charged nothing); an activation that paid keeps its settled record until a later attempt commits it. The subscription client rejects a Challenge whose chain differs from a configured `ChainID`, as the charge client does.
