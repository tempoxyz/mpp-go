Added Zone payments to the Tempo charge method, as in mppx. A server accepts withdrawals from Zones with `Config.Zones` or `ChargeParams.Zones` (`tempo.Zones{IDs: []uint32{3, 7}}`, or `tempo.Zones{Any: true}` for any Zone), advertised as `methodDetails.zones`, and verifies `zone` credentials against the Zone portal's settlement on Tempo. A server refuses a `zone` credential that carries a `source`, since nothing binds it to the withdrawal; mppx reports no payer for one. A client pays from a Zone balance with `Config.Zone`: it withdraws to the recipient with a challenge-bound memo, waits for the withdrawal to settle on Tempo, and sends a `zone` credential with the settlement transaction hash.

If the wait fails, the client returns `*ZoneSettlementPendingError` (or `*ZoneWithdrawalUnknownError` when the withdrawal's receipt cannot be found), carrying the challenge; passing that challenge to the same `Method`'s `CreateCredential` resumes the same withdrawal instead of paying again. A receipt that does not show the signed withdrawal's WithdrawalRequested event (sender, token, recipient, amount and memo) also leaves the outcome unknown. The client checks the challenge's expiry again just before sending a withdrawal and returns ErrZoneChallengeExpiresTooSoon, sending nothing, when preparing it took too long for it to settle in time. The Transport does not pay again after a 402 to a `zone` credential. Zone IDs must be positive and below 421700000, as in mppx, in `Zones`, `zone` credentials and `Zone.ZoneID`, and a Zone's portal address is derived from its literal ID, so a Zone chain ID such as 421700003 is never read as Zone 3.
