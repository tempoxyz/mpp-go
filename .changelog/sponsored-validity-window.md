Widened the sponsored (fee-payer) Tempo charge validity window and enforced the 15 second floor on both sides, to absorb clock skew between clients and servers. The client now sets the expiring-nonce `valid_before` to now + 60 seconds (`tempo.SponsoredValidBeforeWindow`, was 25), computed just before signing and capped at 15 seconds past the second after the challenge expiry, so any credential that reaches the server before the challenge expires keeps the server's 15 second minimum remaining validity; with a long challenge this leaves about 45 seconds for clock skew, signing and round trips. The client refuses, before authorizing or signing, a sponsored challenge that expires less than 15 seconds from now (`ErrSponsoredChallengeExpiresTooSoon`), and its transport skips such a challenge when another one is offered. The Tempo server method refuses to issue a nonzero sponsored charge challenge whose `Expires` is less than 15 seconds after issuance; zero-amount (proof) charges and requests carrying a credential are not affected. `tempo.FeePayerWindow` is deprecated in favour of `tempo.SponsoredValidBeforeWindow`. Clients should upgrade alongside servers.
