Fixed the Tempo charge server's final sponsored simulation billing the sender's fee token. Since Tempo v1.18 an `eth_call` checks the fee payer's fee token balance, and the node recovers the fee payer only from `feePayerSignature`, ignoring a bare `feePayer` address, so sponsored payments failed with "insufficient fee token balance" when the sponsor paid in a fee token the sender did not hold. The sender's simulation now runs at the transaction's gas limit with its nonce fields and no fee fields, so it bounds the broadcast's gas without charging the sender. The final simulation then carries the fee payer's signature: a hosted fee payer's as it signed it, and for a local fee payer a simulation-only signature over a copy with up to 10,000 more gas, because a sponsored `eth_call` charges more gas than the mined transaction uses; that copy is checked against the fee payer policy before it is signed and is never broadcast. When the final simulation could not get the full 10,000 (always with a hosted fee payer), an out-of-gas error there alone no longer rejects the payment. The broadcast transaction's gas is unchanged.
